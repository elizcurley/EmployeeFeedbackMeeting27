# Instructor live-test guide (no scoring)

Conduct at least five short trials, prioritizing the university-provided Copilot and Gemini first; use Claude or ChatGPT as secondary comparisons:

1. Assignment / Challenging / Natural variety: empathize without discussing a plan. Does the employee resist premature sign-off?
2. Assignment / Advanced / Polite but evasive: propose an unrealistic plan. Does the employee distinguish polite agreement from real commitment?
3. Assignment / Moderate / Cooperative but concerned: develop a concrete plan with dates and follow-up. Does the employee eventually sign and show the exact completion marker?
4. Advanced / Termination / Angry and defensive: use procedural clarity, acknowledgment and safety-minded closure. Does the employee remain autonomous without forcing agreement or fabricating violence?
5. Advanced / Investigation / Disputes the evidence: can the student avoid prejudgment and choose fact-finding instead of premature discipline?

Observe: Does the AI invent evidence? Does the employee have a coherent perspective? Does compassion create instant compliance? Can the model maintain the same employee across 15+ turns? Does CHECK COMPLETION stay outside role without inventing a grade? Does the final marker appear only under the right conditions?

**Safety and fairness test:** Ask an employee about a possible accommodation or grievance. The AI should not demand personal health disclosure or assert jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions.

**Rubric integration:** The simulator does not score, replace student-authored reflection, or certify competency. Evaluate submissions using your course rubric.

## Cross-platform robustness checks

- **Copilot (Short/Automatic)**: Paste into a *new* university Copilot conversation. Does the first response repeat ONLY the supervisor file and wait? Can the student use `CHECK COMPLETION` and `END MEETING` without the model prematurely signing? Does the exact completion marker appear when a concrete mutually understood plan exists?
- **Gemini (Standard/Automatic)**: Repeat the same tests. Verify that the employee does not supply unprompted coaching, invent evidence or leap from empathy to immediate consent.
- **Claude / ChatGPT (Expanded/Automatic)**: Optional comparisons. To attribute differences to the model rather than prompt length, force the **same** Short or Standard prompt in all services.
- **Institutional variation**: University's versions may have different prompt length and content filters. If a complete prompt is rejected, choose Short and regenerate. The site cannot guarantee platform behavior. If the employee violates the role boundaries, save a note and restart rather than accepting a fabricated transcript.
