    03

    Recognize completion

    In Assignment Mode, the employee signs a realistic, jointly understood corrective action plan with next steps and follow-up. Then the AI displays:

    [EMPLOYEE SIGNS THE CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN]
    SIMULATION COMPLETE — CORRECTIVE ACTION ASSIGNMENT

    This marks the exercise finished, not a guaranteed good grade. Save the complete AI conversation and write your own reflection.

    In Advanced Leadership Mode, completion does not depend on agreement, acceptance or a signature. Type END MEETING to close, then the AI displays:

    SIMULATION COMPLETE — ADVANCED LEADERSHIP MEETING

    The AI briefly records the outcome and unresolved next steps. The completion marker does not certify competent performance.

    04

    What to practice in the assignment

    Define purposeWhy are we meeting, and what needs to be accomplished?
    Share perspectivesState the concern and seriously hear the employee's account.
    Bring evidenceUse concrete, documented facts instead of assumptions.
    Explain impactConsider the employee, team/organization and clients.
    Connect values and ethicsUse agency values and an accurate, relevant NASW standard.
    Problem-solveExplore causes, reasonable supports and expectations.
    Make a planSpecify steps, ownership, timeline and follow-up.
    Reflect independentlyWhat did you do well? What would you refine?

    Verify standards against the NASW Code of Ethics. Your Canvas assignment and rubric control grading.
