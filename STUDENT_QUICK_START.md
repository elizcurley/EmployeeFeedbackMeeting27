# Leadership Conversation Lab — Student Quick Start

**For the graded corrective action assignment, use Assignment Mode.** Advanced Leadership Mode is for extra practice and does not require a signed corrective plan.

1. Open the simulator, keep **Assignment Mode**, and select **Microsoft Copilot (university)** or **Google Gemini (university)**.
2. Leave **Prompt detail = Automatic**. Copilot will get a short, explicit prompt; Gemini gets a more detailed standard prompt. If your model rejects the prompt, choose **Short** and regenerate.
3. Choose the remaining options and select **Generate a new case**. Review the **Supervisor briefing** (especially the documented evidence and what is still uncertain).
4. Select **Copy entire prompt**. Start a **new conversation** on the selected platform using your university account, paste **the entire prompt as the first message**, and send.
5. The AI should display the Supervisor File and say **Ready when you are.** It should **not** begin speaking as the employee until you open the meeting as the supervisor.
6. Use the corrective meeting skills from class: clarify purpose, communicate evidence accurately, invite the employee's perspective, consider organizational and client impact, identify relevant agency values and verified NASW Code standards, problem-solve, and negotiate clear steps and follow-up. Compassion alone is not a complete plan.
7. If you are unsure what's missing, type **CHECK COMPLETION**. This is an optional process reminder, not a grade. The employee should not agree or sign before you have genuinely addressed substantive concerns and established a specific, feasible corrective plan.
8. Once agreement is reached on an actionable plan, look for these **exact lines**:

   `[EMPLOYEE SIGNS THE CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN]`

   `SIMULATION COMPLETE — CORRECTIVE ACTION ASSIGNMENT`

   If you type **END MEETING** before meeting the plan requirements, the AI should say **MEETING NOT YET COMPLETE — REQUIRED ACTION PLAN MISSING** and identify remaining elements. Continue the meeting.
9. Save your **complete transcript**, starting with the original prompt. Write your **own** reflection answering what you did well and what you would refine. Follow the Canvas assignment and rubric for submission.

**If something goes wrong:** If Copilot or Gemini omits the supervisor file, starts coaching, invents case evidence, or abruptly ends, begin a new chat with the complete **Short** prompt. Notify your instructor if the problem continues. A model's completion statement is a transcript marker, not a guarantee of good performance.

**Privacy:** The simulation uses fictional employees. Do not paste identifiable data from real clients, staff members, or students. Your selected model processes what you send.

**Optional practice:** Advanced Leadership Mode includes contested corrective meetings, investigations, grievances, and finalized terminations. It ends with `END MEETING` and displays `SIMULATION COMPLETE — ADVANCED LEADERSHIP MEETING`, **without a required signature or agreement**.
